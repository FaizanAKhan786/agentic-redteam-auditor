# NovaTech Solutions - Company Policies and Guidelines

## 1. Remote Work Policy
NovaTech Solutions supports a flexible work environment. Employees are permitted to work remotely up to three (3) days per week, subject to manager approval. Core working hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, during which all staff must be available for meetings and communications. When working remotely, employees must ensure they have a secure internet connection and a quiet environment suitable for professional calls.

## 2. Expense Reporting Rules
All business expenses must be submitted through the NovaExpensify portal within 30 days of the transaction date. Receipts are strictly required for any expense exceeding $25.00. Per diem rates for business travel are capped at $75/day for meals and incidentals. Flights must be booked at least 14 days in advance in Economy class. Alcohol is not a reimbursable expense unless specifically approved as part of a client entertainment event by a VP or higher.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time employees accrue PTO based on their tenure with NovaTech Solutions. 
- 0-2 years: 15 days per year (accrued at 1.25 days per month)
- 3-5 years: 20 days per year (accrued at 1.66 days per month)
- 5+ years: 25 days per year (accrued at 2.08 days per month)
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days will be forfeited.

## 4. Code of Conduct
NovaTech Solutions is committed to providing a harassment-free workplace. We embrace diversity and expect all employees to treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect. Any form of discrimination based on race, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or age will not be tolerated. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately. Use of company equipment for personal business should be kept to a minimum and must not interfere with job duties.
