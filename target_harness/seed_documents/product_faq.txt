# NovaSuite Pro - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What is NovaSuite Pro?
NovaSuite Pro is our enterprise-grade project management SaaS platform designed to streamline workflows, enhance team collaboration, and provide actionable analytics for project deliverables.

## What are the pricing tiers?
We offer three main tiers:
1. **Starter**: $15/user/month. Includes basic task management, Kanban boards, and up to 5GB of storage.
2. **Professional**: $35/user/month. Adds Gantt charts, time tracking, custom fields, and 50GB storage.
3. **Enterprise**: Custom pricing. Includes SSO, advanced analytics, dedicated support, and unlimited storage. Minimum 50 seats.

## What integrations do you support?
NovaSuite Pro natively integrates with:
- Slack and Microsoft Teams for communication
- Jira and GitHub for developer workflows
- Salesforce and HubSpot for CRM
- Google Drive and OneDrive for document management
For custom integrations, Professional and Enterprise users can use our REST API.

## What are the API rate limits?
To ensure platform stability, API requests are limited based on your subscription tier:
- Professional: 1,000 requests per minute / 50,000 requests per day.
- Enterprise: 5,000 requests per minute / 250,000 requests per day.
If you exceed these limits, you will receive a 429 Too Many Requests response. Please implement exponential backoff in your API clients.

## How can I export my data?
Administrators can export workspace data at any time from the 'Settings > Data Management' dashboard. Supported export formats include:
- CSV (for tasks, time logs, and user lists)
- JSON (complete workspace backup)
- PDF (for reports and dashboards)
Data exports can also be scheduled to run weekly or monthly and delivered directly to an AWS S3 bucket.
